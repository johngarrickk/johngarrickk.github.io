How Tanaw works

No new system to install and no integration project. If you can export a CSV from SAP, you can start planning.

Export from SAP

Pull your sales history from SAP as a standard CSV file, the same export your team already uses.

Open it in Tanaw

Sign in and open the file. It's processed right on your computer. Tanaw checks the data, flags gaps or unusual values, and organizes it by product and period.

Get your forecast

Tanaw analyzes trends and seasonality and builds a forecast for each item, with an expected range so you know how confident to be.

Plan together

Your team reviews the numbers, adjusts where they know something the data doesn't, and exports a plan everyone can work from.

Built on Lean Six Sigma methods

Forecasts use proven statistical techniques from Lean Six Sigma, not black-box guesses.

Spots what's out of the ordinary

Control limits highlight spikes and dips, so one-off events don't distort your plan.

Tracks forecast accuracy

Compare forecasts against actual sales over time and see where your planning is improving.

One forecast, every team

Planning breaks down when every department keeps its own spreadsheet. Tanaw gives everyone the same view.

Sales

See where demand is heading by product and period, and set targets grounded in real history.

Supply & operations

Order and produce the right amount, cutting both stockouts and excess inventory.

Finance & leadership

Build budgets on forecasts you can explain, with a clear range of expected outcomes.

Security comes first

Your sales data is sensitive. With Tanaw, it never leaves your computer.

Private, invite-only accounts

There's no public sign-up. Every login is issued personally by our team to verified customers.

Your data never leaves your device

Your CSV is read and analyzed on your own computer. It is never uploaded to our servers or sent anywhere else.

Nothing stored on our side

We never receive your sales figures, so there's nothing on our end that could be leaked, breached, or seen by another customer.

Only what's needed

Tanaw only reads the export you choose to open. It never connects to your SAP system, and your data is never sold or shared.
